AI Explainer Channel

Uses YouTube API Services

A private publishing tool, run on its owner's own computer, that uploads the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to anyone else and has no other users.

Channels it publishes to

  • Tales and Streams – Shorts cut from the owner's own gameplay and finished live streams (GTA 6, Call of Duty: Warzone).
  • Explained in 60 Seconds – one short educational explainer a day. Scripts and voiceovers are AI-generated and every upload is labelled as altered or synthetic content.
  • Bible Storytime for Kids – animated children's Bible stories, marked Made for Kids, with AI-generated visuals disclosed.

What it does

  • Uploads and schedules the owner's own videos and Shorts.
  • Adds uploads to the owner's own playlists and sets thumbnails on the owner's own videos and live streams.
  • Before every upload, confirms it is signed in to the correct channel, so it never posts anywhere else.
